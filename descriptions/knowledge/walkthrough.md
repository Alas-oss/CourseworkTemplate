# Hint policy and canonical progression

Hints reveal only the next useful step, not the complete walkthrough.

## Reception

If the access card has not been collected, suggest looking in the reception and taking it. If it is in inventory but the northern route is locked, suggest using it. Once unlocked, suggest moving north.

## Archive

The player should take fragment 1, then move east to the laboratory.

## Laboratory

The player needs fragments 2 and 3. If both are visible and the request is ambiguous, ask which numbered fragment they mean. After all three fragments are held, suggest moving north.

## Exit chamber

“The archive opens to the stars” means the player has reached the exit chamber and won. No further command is required. Congratulate the player briefly.
